## 生成提示词

### 天空

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one complete game stage environment illustration for a 2D pixel-art, humorous casual platform fighting game for 1v1, 2v2 and 4-player free-for-all.
Primary request: SKY theme, a playable-looking floating-island arena above a vast sea of clouds.
Composition: a single wide 16:9 landscape image, strictly orthographic SIDE VIEW. Show the whole arena from a fixed camera. One generously wide flat grass-topped floating stone island across the lower middle, occupying about 75 percent of image width; leave clear fall zones on both sides. Three smaller floating grass-and-stone platforms at distinct higher elevations, two mid-level left/right and one upper central. Their tops are clearly horizontal and separated by easy-to-read jump gaps. Leave generous empty fighting space above each surface and through the central area for four players. All platforms fit within the frame.
Backdrop: layered pale cloud banks, a bright open sky and distant floating landforms. Lively, whimsical environmental shapes, small wind-bent tufts of grass and a few trailing vines beneath islands. Scenic without foreground clutter.
Style: polished, charming 16-bit pixel art with visibly chunky square pixels; a consistent coarse pixel scale across the entire image; restrained dithering, simple clean clusters, crisp stair-step outlines, no antialiasing, no blurry gradients. Intended visual feel of low-resolution pixel art enlarged by an integer factor. Saturated teal-blue sky, cream clouds, fresh grass green, warm ochre rocks. Foreground platforms have dark readable outlines; background has softer contrast.
Constraints: standalone full stage artwork, not a contact sheet, tileset sheet, diagram or screenshot mockup. NO characters, enemies, UI, text, letters, logos, borders or watermark. No isometric perspective, no angled top-down scene, no photographic textures, no smooth vector art. Gameplay surfaces take priority over decorative detail. Landscape 16:9.
